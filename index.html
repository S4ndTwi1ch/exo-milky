Observational Data & Scientific Sources

All orbital mechanics, stellar distances, and physical parameters throughout this atlas are derived from primary peer-reviewed astrophysical surveys. To preserve scientific integrity, observed metrics are strictly differentiated from illustrative visualizations.

Planetary masses ($M_\oplus$), transit radii ($R_\oplus$), orbital semi-major axes ($AU$), equilibrium temperatures, and discovery confirmations sourced from the Planetary Systems (PS) and Composite Parameters (PSCompPars) tables.
